## 1. Design Direction & Concept Manifesto

### Concept Name: *SAME CURIOSITY. BIGGER TOMORROWS.*

Techfest was founded at IIT Bombay in 1998 on an unyielding principle: **restless human curiosity**. From small mechanical prototypes to high-octane 60kg titanium combat robotics, quantum computation, and interplanetary exploration, the underlying drive has remained identical—while the scale of our tomorrows has expanded exponentially.

This garment translates that 29-year evolution into a **luxury technical streetwear hoodie**:
- **Garment Silhouette:** Boxy, drop-shoulder oversized fit inspired by contemporary Tokyo/Berlin technical fashion.
- **Visual Tension:** Clean, restrained, wearable front balanced by a monumental, celestial back statement.
- **Architectural & Space Influences:** Brutalist blueprint schematics of the iconic IIT Bombay Powai campus juxtaposed with Keplerian planetary orbits and aerospace telemetry.
- **Disciplined Palette:** Deep Void Black foundation (`#0B0D10`) with high-legibility Off-White (`#F3F4F6`), supporting Steel Grey (`#6B7280`), and focused, energetic strikes of Tech Blue (`#3B82F6`).

## 4. Front Design Breakdown

1. **Left Chest Identity Crest (90 × 40 mm):**
   - Geometric faceted `TF` monogram housed within an angular hexagonal wireframe reticle.
   - Clean typographic wordmark: `TECHFEST` (Orbitron Bold, 16pt, Off-White `#F3F4F6`).
   - Edition annotation: `// ED.2026-27` (Tech Blue `#3B82F6`) and `IIT BOMBAY CONCEPT LAB`.
   - Executed as a **High-Density 3D Silicone Transfer** (0.8mm relief height) for a tactile, luxurious finish.

2. **Lower Flank & Kangaroo Pocket Architectural Blueprint (160 × 140 mm):**
   - Subtle tone-on-tone blueprint wireframe representing structural cantilever trusses and elevation grid coordinates.
   - Micro-telemetry annotation: `19.1334° N // 72.9133° E [SAC_POWAI]`.
   - Printed with **Matte Water-Based Discharge Ink** to maintain 100% fabric breathability without hand-feel stiffness.

3. **Left Sleeve Slogan (320 × 20 mm):**
   - High-contrast vertical sleeve print: `EXPLORE // BUILD // COLLABORATE // BELONG`.
   - Accompanied by fine registration guide lines in Tech Blue.

4. **Right Sleeve Hem Patch (85 × 24 mm):**
   - Compact military-spec technical badge: `TECHFEST.27` with illuminated blue status dot.

## 9. Figma Import & Collaboration Workflow

The master vector files are built to open seamlessly in **Figma** with preserved layer hierarchies, vector bezier curves, and editable text:

### 3-Step Import Instructions:
1. **Open Figma:** Launch Figma desktop or go to [figma.com](https://www.figma.com/).
2. **Import SVG:** Drag and drop `design/presentation-board.svg` (or `design/front-hoodie.svg` / `design/back-hoodie.svg`) directly onto your Figma canvas.
3. **Inspect & Edit:**
   - Figma automatically generates an organized **2400 × 1600 px** Frame.
   - Text layers (`Orbitron`, `Inter`) remain live and editable.
   - Garment contours, seams, kangaroo pocket welts, and aglets are grouped as discrete vector shapes (`#garment-front`, `#artwork-front`, `#garment-back`, `#artwork-back`).
4. **Enable Editor Access:** Click **Share** in Figma, set General Access to **"Anyone with the link can edit"**, and share the project URL.
